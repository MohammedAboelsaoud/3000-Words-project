# talk | Also, ___ | Conversation words: also = so, well.
[Also], ich finde das gut. | Well, I think it's good.
[Na ja], es geht so. | Well, it's so-so.
[Übrigens], ich komme morgen nicht. | By the way, I'm not coming tomorrow.
[Eigentlich] wollte ich zu Hause bleiben. | Actually I wanted to stay at home.

# talk | Zum Beispiel ___ | Giving examples and adding.
[Zum Beispiel] Äpfel oder Birnen. | For example apples or pears.
Ich mag Obst, [vor allem] Erdbeeren. | I like fruit, especially strawberries.
[Außerdem] ist es billig. | Besides, it's cheap.
[Und dann]? | And then?

# talk | Wie bitte? — Ich meine ___. | Clarifying.
[Ich meine], das ist zu teuer. | I mean, that's too expensive.
[Was meinst du] damit? | What do you mean by that?
[Also noch mal]: morgen um zehn. | So, once again: tomorrow at ten.
[Habe ich das richtig verstanden]? | Did I understand that correctly?

# talk | Da hast du ___. | Agreeing and disagreeing.

[Genau so] ist es. | That's exactly how it is.

[Ich bin nicht sicher]. | I'm not sure.

[Kann sein]. | Could be.

[Mal sehen]. | We'll see.
